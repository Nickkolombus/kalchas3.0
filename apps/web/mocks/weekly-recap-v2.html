Three we got right

Picked because the side we flagged scored, and fast.

12′→13′

Olimpia v Cartaginés

Central American Cup · semi-final

Twelve minutes in and Cartaginés could barely get out of their own half: eleven dangerous attacks to two in five minutes. We posted at 12′. Olimpia scored sixty seconds later.

79′→80′

Noah v Syunik

Armenian Cup · last 16

Two goals down, but playing like the side in front: 51 dangerous attacks to 21 and ten corners. We flagged Noah at 79′. They pulled one back a minute later.

76′→81′

FAR Rabat v IR Tanger

Botola Pro

Seven shots on target, eight more wide, seven corners to one, and still 0–0. On their form FAR should have scored already. It took five more minutes.

The one that got away

Kozakken Boys v Utrecht

KNVB Cup, first round · signal at 75′ · no goal

Our most confident call of the week. Kozakken Boys' recent form said they were two goals short, so we flagged them. But that form was built in the third tier, and this was a cup tie against an Eredivisie side. They had 23% of the ball, and eleven of their fourteen shots missed the target.

Lower-league form doesn't travel. Four Dutch Cup signals this week, no goals.
