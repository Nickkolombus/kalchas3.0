5 · Tag wording

"One side blanks" is the bookmaker market "Both teams to score: No". I'd name the pair after that market so the two read as yes and no of the same thing. While at it, the full set, with my suggested changes highlighted.

PatternTag nowSuggested tagHeadline on the page
Both teams scoreBoth scoreBoth score: YesBoth teams to score looks likely.
At least one team doesn't scoreOne side blanksBoth score: NoOne side could well fail to score.
Both teams to score looks unlikely.
3+ goalsGoalsOver 2.5Expect goals.
0–2 goalsLow scoringUnder 2.5Likely a tight, low-scoring game.

A team rarely keeps a clean sheetLeaky defenceLeaky defence[Team] rarely keep a clean sheet.
Goal after 75'Late goalsLate goalsLeave it open after 75'.
Goal before half-timeFirst-half goalsFirst-half goalsGoals tend to come before half-time.
Long scoring runScoring runScoring run[Team] keep scoring at home.
Short-priced and in formStrong favouriteStrong favourite[Team] are clear favourites, and in form.
Both score: Yes / No (my pick)Same words as the bookmaker market, so bettors know it instantly.
Both score / Not both scoreNo colon, a little clumsier.
Both score / Clean sheet likelyTrue (no goal for one side means a clean sheet for the other) but a different market's name.
